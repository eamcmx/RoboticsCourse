About this site

This is the interactive companion to the live course. Every concept that gets a whiteboard treatment in the lecture also lives here as something you can manipulate — drag the frame, step through the derivation, run the matrix in a browser sandbox. The arc and the worked examples follow the lectures one-for-one, so the site can be used either as a refresher before class or as a self-study path.

Same convention all term: x is red, y is green, z is blue. The mobile-frame axes (u, v, w) are drawn in orange / purple / cyan so they stay visible when they overlap. The default 3D view is azimuth 130°, elevation 30°, like in MATLAB.

Three deliverables, every homework: a derivation by hand, a MATLAB program, and a short video where you explain both. The video isn't optional — it's how I know the work is yours, and it's the assessment.
